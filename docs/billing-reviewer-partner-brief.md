### Review checklist and acceptance record

For each URL record the exact page version/commit, passages checked, governing source and effective date, correction needed, decision, reviewer, and approval date. A checked source link is not independent reviewer approval.

- Confirm code descriptions, applicable service year, payer/setting/locality qualifications, and reimbursement illustrations against current primary sources.
- Check eligibility, consent, initiating visits, staff roles/supervision, time and communication requirements, documentation, overlap and concurrent-service restrictions.
- Check that software outreach is not represented as independently creating qualifying clinical work, billable minutes or payment eligibility.
- Check every synthetic example is clearly labelled, internally consistent, and not represented as a claim approval.
- Verify FAQs and structured data agree with the approved visible content.
- Identify outdated source passages and distinguish proposal from final policy.
- Approve only reviewed pages and passages. Do not extend a limited review to the whole website.
- Obtain explicit consent for the exact public attribution sentence, credentials and any biography link. Publish reviewer metadata only after approval, and re-review when material rules/content change.

## Partner/customer implementation evidence

Target question: “What evidence should our practice examine before changing its outreach workflow?” Reuse the existing implementation guide and case-study methodology rather than publishing another thin landing page.

Needed before a specific customer story is drafted:

| Evidence | Owner supplies | Publication guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Permission | Organization contact and written scope of permission | No name/logo/quote without explicit approval |
| Workflow | Before/after process, retained staff tasks, exception handoff and coverage | Distinguish supported features from custom or planned work |
| Cohort | Inclusion rules, observation dates, active versus cumulative population | Use approved aggregate data only; no patient records |
| Measures | Definitions, numerator/denominator, source exports and measurement period | No fabricated percentages, causality or ROI |
| Limitations | Missing data, selection bias, other simultaneous changes, failure cases | Put limitations beside the result, not only in a footnote |
| Attribution | Actual approved quote and relationship/disclosures | No invented testimonial or independent-review claim |
| Distribution | Named partner/editor and approved draft | No outreach or publication until recipient and material are approved |

Proposed outline for the evidence-backed account:

1. Practice context and the specific operational problem.
2. Staff-led workflow before implementation.
3. Software-supported steps, retained clinical responsibilities and exception handling.
4. Pilot evaluation method and approved aggregate results, if available.
5. Limitations, what did not work, and what another practice should test.
6. Links to the relevant existing guide and a clearly described workflow demo.

If results are not approved, publish no outcome claims. A neutral evaluation checklist can be prepared instead, explicitly not a customer case study.
