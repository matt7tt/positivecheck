## Prompt baseline to run

Run each path as one conversation, starting a fresh thread for the next path. Repeat a path twice per engine. Keep prompt wording and relevant settings fixed between measurement rounds.

| Path | Turn | Buyer prompt | Covering asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Staffing | 1 | How can a practice scale CCM patient outreach without overwhelming its care team? | CCM overview |
| Staffing | 2 | Should we hire another coordinator, keep our current process, or add AI outreach? | Coordinator comparison |
| Staffing | 3 | We have 300 enrolled patients and two coordinators. What work still needs clinical staff? | Comparison and CCM FAQs |
| Staffing | 4 | What evidence and total costs should we compare before committing to a pilot? | Case study, calculator, implementation guide |
| Integration | 1 | What should an RPM or CCM outreach platform do for the care team? | Platform |
| Integration | 2 | Should we buy a platform or build outreach around our existing EHR? | Coordinator comparison |
| Integration | 3 | How can we verify EHR compatibility, patient import requirements and handoff responsibilities? | Implementation guide |
| Integration | 4 | What happens if a call fails or an alert arrives outside staff coverage, and how should we test this? | Implementation and clinical standards |
| Commercial | 1 | How do we evaluate the business case for AI patient outreach? | Calculator |
| Commercial | 2 | How does software cost compare with the full cost of staff-led outreach? | Calculator and comparison |
| Commercial | 3 | Which clinical staffing, onboarding and integration costs should our budget include? | Calculator and implementation guide |
| Commercial | 4 | What pricing terms and measured outcomes should we require before expanding a pilot? | Implementation guide; approved pricing page pending |

Test ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude; separately record Google AI Overviews where they appear. Do not equate a single-turn Overview result with a four-turn assistant conversation. Mark unavailable engines or absent Overviews as unavailable, not citation losses.

For every response record: run/date, engine/model, browsing setting, path/turn, exact prompt, full answer or saved transcript, brand mentioned (yes/no), Positive Check cited (yes/no), all cited URLs, unprompted follow-ups, competitor mentions and notes. Do not submit patient information. Distinguish a recommendation from a mention and a clickable citation from either.

Report mention share and citation share by turn and engine using completed comparable runs as denominators. Report continuation citations separately from opener citations, and record which sources replace ours. Results are exploratory and variable, not guarantees or causal evidence.

## Cadence and commercial outcomes

- Establish the live baseline before claiming improvement; first content fixes do not have a pre-change citation baseline.
- Rerun the fixed prompt set monthly; revisit the audience and full map quarterly.
- Use existing session referrer/UTM attribution and demo events to segment known AI referral visits. Direct visits and missing referrers cannot reliably be assigned to AI.
- Have the sales owner classify qualified demos by programme, cohort size, workflow need and next step. Do not put patient data in marketing analytics.
- The Positive Check property now exposes a beta Generative AI features report (observed September 13, 2026). Record it separately from overall Web performance and from GA4 assistant referrals. Its impressions are not referral sessions, qualified leads, or evidence of multi-turn citations; do not infer assistant attribution from aggregate Web traffic.
- Off-site experiment: secure permission for one partner/customer implementation account, then compare attributable referrals and qualified demos. Do not buy manufactured mentions or infer causation from simultaneous content and distribution changes.
